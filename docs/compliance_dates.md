# Compliance dates: federal and Illinois (as of Nov 3, 2026)

Half-page reference for the app text, letters, and scorecards. Dates change; recheck the
sources before quoting them to residents.

## Federal (EPA)

| Date | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 16, 2024 | Initial service line inventory due; notification of service line material (lead, GRR, unknown) begins, then annually. Retained from the 2021 LCRR. | [EPA fact sheet][epa-dates] |
| Dec 30, 2024 | Lead and Copper Rule Improvements (LCRI) effective (published Oct 30, 2024, 89 FR 86418). | [Federal Register][fr] |
| Dec 31, 2026 | Annual LCRR notices to customers with lead, GRR, or unknown lines for 2026. | [CDM Smith][cdm], [120Water][120w] |
| Nov 1, 2027 | LCRI compliance date: baseline inventory, service line replacement plan, and school/child-care list due to the state. Mandatory replacement begins. Action level drops to 10 ppb. | [EPA fact sheet][epa-dates], [Federal Register][fr] |
| Dec 31, 2037 | All lead and GRR lines replaced (10% per year average) for most systems. Systems where 10% per year would exceed 39 replacements per 1,000 connections get a deferred deadline. | [Federal Register][fr], [NewGen][newgen] |

The LCRI is being challenged in court (AWWA v. EPA, D.C. Circuit); oral argument was
scheduled for Sep 30, 2026. EPA has said the rule stays in effect during review ([CDM Smith][cdm]).

## Illinois

| Date | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Apr 15, 2022 / Apr 15, 2023 | Initial material inventory developed / submitted to Illinois EPA. | [Illinois EPA][iepa], [415 ILCS 5/17.12(d)][ilcs] |
| Apr 15, 2024 | Complete material inventory and initial replacement plan due. | [Illinois EPA][iepa], [IML 2024][iml24] |
| Apr 15 each year, 2025–2027 | Updated replacement plan; final plan due Apr 15, 2027. | [IML 2024][iml24] |
| 2027 onward (state law) | Replacement at a rate set by inventory size: 100,000 or more lead lines means at least 2% per year, up to 50 years. | [415 ILCS 5/17.12(v)][ilcs] |
| Jan 22, 2026 / Feb 17, 2026 | Illinois Pollution Control Board adopted the LCRI without changes; the rules were published in the Illinois Register. Illinois EPA gives the state compliance date as Nov 27, 2027. | [IML][iml-lcri], [IEPA advisory board minutes, Apr 8, 2026][iepa-min] |

## What this means for Chicago

- Chicago's April 2025 replacement plan follows the state 50-year track (about 8,300 lines a year,
  finishing in 2076). The federal deferred schedule works out to about 20 years, close to
  20,000 lines a year from 2027 ([Chicago Sun-Times][sun], [Inside Climate News][icn]).
- Illinois EPA told its advisory board that the state act requires the most stringent rule, so the
  LCRI replacement rate and deadline apply ([IEPA minutes][iepa-min]).
- For the app: say "federal rules require replacement starting in late 2027" and link these
  sources; do not state a specific completion year for an individual address.
