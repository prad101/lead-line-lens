What residents can do

  • "Unknown" means the city has no record. Treat it as possibly lead until it is checked.
  • Look up your address on the city's service line map.
  • Get a free water test kit: call 311 or visit chicagowaterquality.org.
  • Homeowners with lower incomes may qualify for a free replacement through the Equity Lead Service Line Replacement Program.
  • Lead is most harmful to young children and pregnant people (U.S. EPA).
